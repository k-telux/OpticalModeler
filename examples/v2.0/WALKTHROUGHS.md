# Six complete interaction walkthroughs

[中文版本](WALKTHROUGHS.zh-CN.md) · [Case index and real public images](README.md)

All dialogue below is edited and paraphrased for teaching. Cases B–E summarize private review workflows through functional aliases, without publishing the underlying setup or claiming their files are downloadable here. Case F illustrates the fail-closed evidence contract and tested ledger behavior. A final review handoff is a concrete result, even when physical qualification remains incomplete.

## A. From a public 2D schematic to the delivered 3D presentation

**Input:** The existing public G1/G2 schematic. **Operation:** Reconstruction, followed by a separate editorial presentation. **Required output:** Complete apparatus views, interface evidence and status-consistent handoff.

**User:** Use this diagram to build the complete optical table. Include the sample/collection path, fiber coupling and both counting outputs. I want a clear overview and a top view.

**Agent:** I will map every directed branch to a device, optical port and support path before placement. Free-space light, guided fiber and electrical detector links will be separate families. I will preserve your diagram as authority and mark any substitute or modeled component.

**User:** The objective is facing the wrong way, the coils should be directly on the main table, and some clamps overlap. Correct those issues before polishing the render.

**Agent:** I have recorded those corrections against the affected families and frozen the previous package. I will first prove one launch with the large entrance toward free space, the real retaining seat and continuous path to the fiber interface. Then I will propagate the corrected assembly, move the complete coil families and recheck all clamp neighbors after reopening the saved scene.

**User:** Now make the accepted geometry suitable for a main figure or cover, but keep the apparatus and beam topology fixed.

**Agent:** I will use a separate presentation copy and retain bright interface views. I will verify actual final dimensions, inspect the hero/top images and compare the saved geometry before the final handoff.

**Final-result example:** The historical public result is available as the [hero preview](../g1g2/output/v18_nature_hero_graphite_final_4k_preview.jpg), [annotated top preview](../g1g2/output/v18_nature_complete_top_annotated_final_4k_preview.jpg) and [sanitized acceptance record](../g1g2/evidence/v18_nature_final_acceptance.json). That record reports `PASS_V18_NATURE_FINAL_VERIFIED`, saved-scene reopen, image QA and three publication reviews. The files here are compressed previews; the editable scene and original 16-bit masters are excluded.

**What to reuse:** The topology/interface/support/evidence process. For a different measurement at this quality, start a new topology and empty scene. The historical final image cannot certify the new instrument.

## B. Photograph reconstruction with unknown hardware details

**Input:** Annotated private photos, a functional sketch and an accepted baseline. **Aliases:** `SOURCE_A`, `SOURCE_B`, `DELAY_STAGE`, `SAMPLE_MODULE`, `SPECTROGRAPH`, `DETECTOR_BACK`. **Required output:** One usable complete review model before further optional research.

**User:** Keep the accepted modeling rules and rebuild our photographed pump/probe apparatus on that baseline. Some instrument shapes are right, but their positions and orientations are wrong. Finish an editable model I can inspect.

**Agent:** I will preserve the accepted files and create a new revision. I will separate photographed/user-confirmed installed identities from manufacturer candidates and photo-estimated geometry. I will check all supplied views before asking about consequential unknowns, and keep electrical cables outside this optics-only scene.

**User:** The two height-changing mirror pairs are actually mounted on the same upright perforated board. The model only has unsupported mirror arms.

**Agent:** I will connect the real holder/mount families to that board and its bracket/base/table chain. Adding a decorative plate behind the existing arms would not close the support issue. I will also move each large instrument with its ports, supports and fasteners, then recompute its affected optical connections.

**User:** I do not have the remaining focal lengths tonight. Finish this review version first and list what I should check in the laboratory.

**Agent:** I will complete the authorized review candidate without inventing prescriptions or exact installed variants. The guide will distinguish measured model geometry from laboratory measurements. Unknown internal instrument optics will remain a functional black box rather than an invented ray train.

**Agent, after checks:** I reopened the saved model, checked the affected paths and support/neighbor scope, and inspected actual overview, top and interface images. A readable opening workspace is saved. Any unavailable real-input UI test remains separately unverified; rendering alone does not prove navigation.

**Final-result example:** "The photographed-layout review package is ready: an editable optics-only model, full-path images, a short viewing guide and a measurement checklist. The accepted baseline is preserved. Installed variant, hidden supports, lens prescriptions, threads/preload and whole-system performance remain unknown; the model verdict is `PARTIAL_SCOPED`, with physical approval false."

**Handoff contents:** `Delivery/Review.blend`, requested final `Images/`, a viewing/measurement guide and a scope/hash manifest. These names specify a private handoff; this repository does not contain those laboratory files.

**Next visit:** Photograph unread labels and mounting interfaces with a scale; record optical-center heights, relay separations and the detector/adapter configuration. Ask for those facts after delivering the review candidate when that is the user's chosen order.

## C. A compact-layout request that must survive user rejection

**Input:** Accepted lower-branch layout, fixed upper endpoint and annotated direction arrows. **Operation:** Constrained correction. **Risk:** Treating "compact" as permission for a new optical design.

**User:** Move this source branch closer to the upper platform. You can adjust the four in-plane mirrors and rotate the upright board as shown. Keep the source/conditioning arrangement.

**Agent:** I will lock the source-group internal vectors, the optical order, the horizontal conditioning direction and the accepted upper endpoint. I will enumerate permitted mirror/support moves and measure actual board/table clearances before the preview.

**User:** The candidate is wrong. You changed the branch arrangement and moved the upper reflector. I only wanted the source group translated and the board adapted to the fixed upper route.

**Agent:** The geometric checks did not establish your intent. I will restore the accepted upper pose and original group relationships, keep this rejection in the issue record, and correct only the authorized degrees of freedom. Your newer annotation supersedes the rejected layout, not unrelated baseline geometry.

**User:** Lock the original upper position. The board and its two mirrors may slide along that fixed incident axis while keeping their working faces outward.

**Agent:** I will move the board and supported mirrors coherently along that axis, preserve their heights/normals, and reanchor the affected fasteners to real holes. I will not add an assumed platform aperture. The next review will compare the fixed endpoint and pairwise source vectors explicitly.

**User:** The device relationships are now correct, but the overall translation is still insufficient. Put the complete lower branch, except the upright board, under the upper platform. You may extend the upper plate and relocate its large supports to avoid obstruction.

**Agent:** This adds a footprint requirement. I will measure each complete device/support family against the actual upper-plate projection, apply one coherent translation and use only the permitted mirror/support exceptions. Centers inside the projection are insufficient. Changed-to-changed neighbors and relocated column/foot/bolt anchors are included in the saved-model checks.

**Agent, after checks:** The full declared family footprints are covered by the plate projections, and the affected state paths have no new unexpected opaque hits in the audited scope. Lower/height-transfer views were checked in actual pixels; a blocked camera was replaced by a complementary angle without hiding apparatus.

**Final-result example:** "The requested complete translation and platform coverage are delivered in a new engineering model, separate presentation copy, final images and correction guide. The previously accepted files remain exact. The record shows retained source-group relationships and fixed upper optical poses. Real retention/preload, complete load qualification and installed prescriptions remain unverified; `PARTIAL_SCOPED` is retained."

**What prevents recurrence:** An intent table alongside the geometry audit: fixed upper pose, rigid internal vectors, exact mirror order, retained horizontal segment, actual full-footprint coverage and authorized support exceptions. Do not report the rejected candidate as accepted because its numerical diagnostics passed.

## D. Darker enclosures and readable beams without geometry drift

**Input:** Accepted geometry, existing camera set and a screenshot of pale enclosures. **Operation:** Presentation only. **Required output:** A separate copy with all affected views freshly rendered.

**User:** The model is correct. The power detector enclosure is too white, and the source lids have broad pale reflections. Make the overall lighting slightly darker so the current beams read clearly.

**Agent:** I will freeze the original and use a new presentation copy. I will first identify the actual shell/slot materials and their shared users. Detector sensing faces, optical coatings, beam geometry/colors/radii, visibility and existing cameras stay fixed.

**Agent, after diagnosis:** A material named "black" has a pale base color, and a lid shader is shared with other objects. I will give only the intended enclosure/lid slots independent material copies and reduce their glare with scoped shader/light changes.

**User:** Keep this preview's tone. Do not start another palette campaign.

**Agent:** I will retain this selection. Before final rendering, I will compare saved source/candidate geometry, topology, normals, present attributes, modifiers, semantic metadata and both operating states. Matching object counts alone would not prove preservation.

**Agent, after checks:** The protected geometry and cameras match, with only the declared shell material overrides and lighting changes. I rendered every affected final view, decoded their native dimensions/bit depth and inspected the actual images. Lower-level device edges and branch routes remain visible.

**Final-result example:** "The controlled-gray presentation package is frozen: a new editable presentation file, eight native 3840 × 2400 RGB16 images, a short viewing note and manifest. The original engineering/presentation files are preserved. This changes appearance only and grants no new physical qualification. `reply_visual_confirmation=incomplete` if this environment cannot capture the rendered final reply."

**Image-provenance rule:** A changed camera alone may permit retaining unaffected images with their original producer and a complete dependency bridge. A relight/recolor affects their dependencies, so those views need fresh rendering. Never replace the receipt's producer hash to make an old image look newly rendered.

## E. A proposed detector, two selected exits and an entrance shutter

**Input:** Existing instrument/photos, official configuration evidence and an accepted gray presentation. **Aliases:** `SPECTROGRAPH`, `DETECTOR_BACK`, `DETECTOR_SIDE_PROPOSED`, `ENTRANCE_SHUTTER`. **Operation:** Local reference reconstruction, not manufacturer or hardware certification.

**User:** Keep this version. Recheck the spectrograph: it has one entrance and two exits. Our back detector is installed; we plan another camera at the side. The external entrance shutter is missing from the model.

**Agent:** I will freeze the accepted body, entrance, back-detector poses and appearance. I will reconcile the official port terminology with the current model coordinates and inspect the real openings. The side detector will be labeled proposed; unknown adapter, focal-plane and shutter details remain explicit.

**Agent:** Should the planned detector be included as a clearly marked reference model, or should this version only reserve its interface?

**User:** Include the proposed reference model.

**Agent:** I will add the external opening and reference receiver/support, plus the entrance shutter's open/closed geometry. The configuration evidence confirms selected outputs, so I will not depict both exits as simultaneous splitting. The undocumented internal transfer remains a black box.

**Agent, after first reopen:** The axis reaches the modeled detector window, but that does not prove all mounting interfaces. Finite sections exposed an entrance cap/profile mismatch at a reference fastener. I will correct that local interface and recheck from entry to hole bottom; nominal radius or bounding-box overlap alone would miss it.

**Agent, after correction:** Five explicit states are recorded: two preserved back-open operating configurations, proposed-side-open, back-closed and proposed-side-closed. Each evaluated state binds selector/visibility/shutter/ray evidence. Closed-state axes stop at the labeled closure envelope and neither output route is active. Unaffected apparatus states and accepted visual settings are preserved.

**Final-result example:** "The local three-port/shutter review package is ready: an editable stateful model, overall and back/side/interface/shutter views, plus a guide and measurement checklist. The back detector remains installed; the side camera is visibly proposed. Finite reference contacts are checked, while true shutter identity, adapters, focal plane, seal, threads/preload and complete load/performance claims remain unverified. The package is for manual review, with physical approval false."

**Useful follow-up:** Confirm the actual port-selector configuration, shutter label and mounting, planned camera variant/cooling, adapter interface and focal depth. A detailed reference model should not silently become an installed-hardware claim.

## F. Audit failure and ledger rejection still produce an honest result

**Input:** A whole-system run whose process exited or whose next event is invalid. **Operation:** Evidence recovery. **Required output:** A valid current record or a reproducible blocker, never a retroactive PASS.

**User:** The background process exited successfully. Finish the workflow and package the result.

**Agent:** I will inspect the fresh report and actual opened-scene identity. Exit zero alone does not establish the requested audit. The report must match the run, attempt nonce, runtime/helper and saved file; the required commit/reopen evidence must also exist.

**Agent:** The current report is missing or mismatched, so this gate is `BLOCKED_AUDITOR_EXECUTION_FAILURE`. The previous generation-only model cannot receive audit credit. I will preserve the failed attempt, diagnose the missing evidence and write the exact blocker/next action.

**User:** Repair the report path and continue the same run. Preserve valid upstream evidence.

**Agent:** I will invalidate the affected stage under the same writer and source lineage, then rerun only dependent checks. If the new ledger event has a duplicate ID or invalid timestamp, candidate replay must reject it before changing either persistent file.

**Agent, after the software check:** The regression check rejects invalid timestamps and duplicate/empty IDs without changing the existing event/state bytes, and the valid record/invalidation path remains replayable. This is precommit validation; it does not claim crash-atomic two-file persistence or new physical-model evidence.

**Final-result example:** "The software ledger correction is verified. If the scene audit still cannot produce matching fresh evidence, the handoff contains the immutable candidate, structured blocker, verified completed upstream gates and next reproducible command; final physical/release approval stays false. Once matching audit evidence is produced, only its actual scope can advance."

The runnable [ledger tests](../../tests/test_workflow_ledger.py) and [runtime validation tests](../../tests/test_validation_runtime.py) cover the software boundary. They do not substitute for Blender geometry, UI interaction or hardware tests.

Blocking/fail-closed findings:

- BVH: `110` blocking collision pairs remain; mechanical assembly does not pass.

- Official CAD semantic ports, optical faces/coatings, threads, fits, clamping, torque, CCR internal path, M_H3 wavelength behavior, detector response, and 633→810 phase mapping remain unverified.
- DMLP735B-to-LMR1/M is a known envelope mismatch; the active retainer is modeled.
- Modeled/surrogate equivalence and paper performance transfer remain blocked.
- Vendor CAD, derived meshes, Blend, and renders remain private-only and excluded.

Required next action: independent read-only audit of this stable public snapshot. Do not claim final/release/publish and do not continue without new approval.


# Explicit exclusions

The following are deliberately absent from this public candidate:

- all 54 official Thorlabs STEP downloads and any vendor CAD archive;
- every STL/mesh derivative generated from vendor CAD;
- both generated and audited Blend files, because they contain vendor-derived meshes;
- the four official Thorlabs CAD drawing PDFs; only their official URLs, byte counts, hashes, retrieval times, and redistribution decision are retained;
- the Blender runtime ZIP and extracted binaries;
- internal blocked-attempt Blend files and reports;
- private absolute paths, installed skills, project memory, Cairn notes, and unrelated prior project artifacts.

The N15 experimental chamber, generic joining fasteners, and load-link witnesses are modeled non-Thorlabs objects. They appear only in raster evidence and metadata and are never counted as official catalog components.

Final/release approval and literal-paper-performance validation are `BLOCKED` pending a new supervisor decision.
